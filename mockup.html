Wins Above Replacement

2025 · through Week 12
Step 1

Price a win

Regress season wins on point differential to fit points-per-win (≈36), so EPA converts to wins with a measured constant rather than folklore.

Step 2

Split the credit

A mixed-effects model on every play with random effects for QB, ball carrier, receiver, line unit and defense. Small samples shrink toward zero automatically.

Step 3

Set replacement

Replacement is measured, not assumed: the pooled per-snap value of players outside the top N by snaps at each position.

Step 4

Publish the error bars

A block bootstrap over drives gives every player an 80% interval. If two players overlap, the page says so instead of ranking them.
